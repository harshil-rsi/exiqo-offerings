Who is G&A Partners — and what are we building for them?
G&A is a leading Professional Employer Organization (PEO). Under a co-employment model it runs payroll, benefits, HR, workers' comp and risk for its clients' employees. Revenue = worksite-employee count × PEPM fee, plus arbitrage on benefits & workers'-comp programs. Onboarding · Improvement Areas
Compress the cycle & kill the coordination tax — target 10–14 days faster
Most compression is achievable without fundamental process redesign — a mix of automated SLAs, document AI, and integration removes the coordination tax that dominates PM and PA time.
AI CSA Extraction & Cross-Validation
Document AI extracts structured fields from the CSA PDF on upload — fee structure, services, SUTA coverage, billing exceptions — and auto-flags discrepancies against ClientSpace handoff data.
Today: CSA reviewed 4× by hand → Target: 1 validation; auto-flag discrepancies to Sales PRD Priority
Mock-Payroll Pre-Flight Validation
Pre-flight engine checks PrismHR config completeness — WC codes, SUTA rates, billing rules, pay-group alignment — before initialization.
Today: iterative calculate-fail-fix loop → Target: pass/fail report with remediation before mock PRD Priority
Automated Data-Collection SLAs & Escalation
Hard data deadlines with automated escalating reminders replace the open-ended, unenforced wait that is the single most variable phase in the cycle.
Today: 10–20 day open-ended wait → Target: day-5/10/15 auto-escalation Automation
Automated PM Workflows
Auto-sync case status across ClientSpace, PrismHR, and the Master Client List on phase events; auto-generate task notes and departmental follow-ups.
Today: status triple-entered across ClientSpace/Prism/Excel → Target: auto-sync on phase events Automation
AI Client Communication & Status Compilation
